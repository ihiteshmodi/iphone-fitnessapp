# Impact map: Phase C — Body Layout, Deliberate Cut Mode, Meal GL & Amino Acids, Measurement Units, Multivitamin Stack Optimizer & Guided Bloodwork

**Why.** Using the app day to day exposed nine friction points across Body, Diet, Progress, Supplements, More, and Bloodwork: important cards were buried, cutting rules could trigger without a deliberate cut, amino acids were checked per day instead of per protein meal, glycemic load was missing, skinfold sites lacked millimeter units, More had manual data-loader buttons, multivitamins hid zero-value nutrients and couldn't be stacked/optimized, and Bloodwork lacked visual range bars, starred mandatory tests, and a plain-language quick-start guide.

**Who.** Hitesh, using one iPhone offline (with his personal SQLite database `fitness.sqlite` bundled into his IPA).

**Goal.** Move from hunting through dense screens and manual setup buttons to fast, visual, meal-accurate and unit-accurate decisions — while keeping personal health data strictly inside the local/bundled SQLite file and out of GitHub.

| ID | Capability | Feature | Status Audit | What Hitesh gets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | body | Bloodwork card placement | Needs small UI move | `Bloodwork` card sits right below *What your weight says* (`weightCard`) and above *Recent weigh-ins* & *Recent calories*. |
